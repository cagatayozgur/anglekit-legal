AngleKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

AngleKit lets you choose a photo and generate the same subject seen from different camera angles. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What we process

When you tap Generate, the photo you selected is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our backend, which forwards it to a third-party AI image provider (fal.ai) to render the new angle. The generated image is returned to the app.

What we store

We do not keep accounts and we do not operate a user database. Photos you submit and the images generated from them are processed transiently to fulfill your request and are not retained by us after processing. We do not use your photos to train models and we do not sell or share them with anyone other than the image provider needed to perform the generation. Any image you choose to save stays in your device's Photos library under your control.

Third-party processing

Image generation is performed by fal.ai. Your submitted photo is transmitted to fal.ai solely to generate your requested result, subject to their terms and privacy practices.

Analytics and tracking

AngleKit contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

Children

AngleKit is not directed to children under 13.
